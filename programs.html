Programs
Pretrial programs are assigned based on risk level, case type, and supervision needs. Each program includes structured compliance requirements and monitored participation.
Authorized Program Structures
STOP-ARES®™
Electronic monitoring and supervision for high-risk defendants requiring location tracking and enforcement.
Cost: $995.00 (Daily Monitoring Schedule)
PASS®™
Substance abuse supervision including testing, counseling, and recovery compliance.
Cost: $1,475.00 (Inpatient) | $2,028.00 (Outpatient)
MARCH®™
Mental health evaluation and structured rehabilitation for behavioral and psychological stabilization.
Cost: $1,226.00 | $1,661.00 | $3,336.00 | $4,885.00
SAFER®™
Domestic and family intervention program focused on accountability and behavioral correction.
Cost: $844.00 (Inpatient) | $1,017.00 (Outpatient)
SOBER-STEP®™
DUI/DWI-focused supervision with sobriety monitoring and driving accountability.
Cost: $845.00 | $1,828.00
RARE-COVA©™
Intensive supervision for repeat offenders focusing on behavioral correction and stability.
Cost: $995.00 | $1,599.00
EARN-ETHICS®™
Financial and ethical rehabilitation program targeting theft-related offenses and accountability.
Cost: $425.00 | $740.00
Program Assignment Notice
All program placements are determined by pretrial officers and are subject to court approval. Participation is mandatory once assigned and must be maintained for continued release eligibility.
