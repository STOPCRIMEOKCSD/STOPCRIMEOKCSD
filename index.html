This portal provides official information regarding pretrial program participation, detainee case status, supervision requirements, transportation coordination, and financial processing procedures.
Pretrial Services Overview
Oklahoma County Pretrial Services coordinates the structured release and monitoring of eligible detainees prior to court proceedings. Services include program assignment, compliance supervision, and administrative case tracking.
Portal Usage
• View authorized pretrial programs
• Review case eligibility and financial obligations
• Understand transportation procedures
• Access approved payment methods
• Contact assigned pretrial personnel
Notice
This website is informational only. All case decisions, financial approvals, and release actions are conducted by authorized pretrial officers and county personnel.
