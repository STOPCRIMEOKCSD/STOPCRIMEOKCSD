Agreement Overview
This page outlines the legal terms, compliance requirements, and operational procedures governing all pretrial program participation, financial transactions, supervision activities, and release coordination conducted under STOPCRIME©™ Pretrial Services.
These terms apply to all defendants, cosigners, guarantors, and client representatives engaging with pretrial services and bond-related financial processing.
Pretrial Program Participation Terms
• All program placements are subject to review and approval by authorized pretrial officers
• Participants must comply with all supervision conditions, including monitoring, reporting, and behavioral requirements
• Failure to comply may result in revocation of release and re-detention
Program structures are enforced in accordance with state pre-conviction supervision policies and judicial directives.
Financial Responsibility Agreement
• All payments made are legally recognized financial obligations tied to the defendant’s release and supervision
• The cosigner/guarantor assumes responsibility for ensuring payment completion and accuracy
• All submitted funds are processed through authorized financial channels and must clear prior to execution of release
Payments are non-refundable once posted to a county holding account, except where governed by applicable law or administrative review.
Payment Compliance & Verification
• All transactions must include verified case credentials and identifiers
• Identity verification is required for all payment methods
• Digital, cash, and terminal payments must follow assigned processor instructions
• Fraudulent transactions, misrepresentation, or unauthorized payment attempts will result in immediate suspension and potential legal referral
Supervision & Monitoring Compliance
• Defendants may be subject to electronic monitoring, substance testing, or structured program participation
• Location tracking and reporting requirements must be followed at all times
• Any violation may result in immediate enforcement action
Liability & Legal Disclaimer
STOPCRIME©™ and associated agencies operate under applicable state, county, and federal frameworks. The department does not provide legal advice and assumes no liability for:

• User error during payment processing
• Incorrect detainee or case information submission
• Failure to follow instructions provided by authorized personnel
• Delays caused by financial institutions or processing systems
Acknowledgment & Consent
By participating in any pretrial program, submitting payment, or engaging in release coordination, the involved party acknowledges:

• They understand and accept all listed terms and conditions
• All actions are taken voluntarily and without coercion
• They assume responsibility for compliance with all procedures and requirements
