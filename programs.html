Authorized Pretrial Program Structures
1. STOP-ARES®™ PROGRAM
(Standard Transition Order of Pretrial Ankle-Monitoring Release & Enforcement Supervision)
High-risk electronic monitoring program focused on location tracking, compliance enforcement, and ensuring court appearance for defendants requiring elevated supervision.
$995.00 (In-House Daily 1–6PM M-F)
2. PASS®™ PROGRAM
(Pretrial Addictive Substance Submission & Supervision Program)
Substance abuse supervision program providing counseling, drug/alcohol testing, and recovery-based rehabilitation to reduce addiction-related offenses.
$1,475.00 (Inpatient) / $2,028.00 (Outpatient)
3. MARCH®™ PROGRAM
(Mental Assessment, Rehabilitation & Correctional Health Program)
Mental health evaluation and rehabilitation program focused on psychological stability, behavioral correction, and structured treatment services.
$1,226.00 (Inpatient) / $1,661.00 (Outpatient) / $3,336.00 (Involuntary Inpatient) / $4,885.00 (Long-term Intensive Inpatient - 24Ms)
4. SAFER®™ PROGRAM
(Structural Accountability & Actions for Family Ethics and Relational Rehabilitation)
Domestic and family violence intervention program emphasizing accountability, behavioral reform, and relational rehabilitation.
$844.00 (Inpatient) / $1,017.00 (Outpatient)
5. SOBER-STEP®™ PROGRAM
(Structured Offender Behavior Evaluation & Rehabilitation for Safe Transportation Enforcement & Prevention)
DWI/DUI-focused program centered on sobriety monitoring, driving accountability, and substance education.
$845.00 (Outpatient) / $1,828.00 (Intense Outpatient Supervision)
6. RARE-COVA©™ PROGRAM
(Repeat Arrestee Rehabilitation, Education, Employment, & Correction of Violent Actions)
Intensive supervision program for repeat violent offenders, combining behavioral correction, employment structure, and recidivism reduction strategies.
$995.00 (Inpatient) / $1,599.00 (Outpatient)
7. EARN-ETHICS®™ PROGRAM
(Economic Accountability and Active Rehabilitation & Navigation for Ethical Teachings, Health Maintenance, & Interventive Correctional Studies)
Theft and financial-motivated offense program focused on ethical decision-making, employment stability, financial accountability, and behavioral correction.
$425.00 (Inpatient) / $740.00 (Outpatient)
