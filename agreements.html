Agreement Overview
This page establishes the governing terms, compliance standards, and operational procedures applicable to all individuals engaging with Oklahoma County Pretrial Services, including detainees, guarantors, and authorized representatives.
Engagement in any pretrial process, payment submission, program participation, or release coordination constitutes acknowledgment and acceptance of the terms contained herein.
Pretrial Program Participation Terms
• Program placement is determined by authorized pretrial personnel based on risk, charge classification, and court directives
• Participants must comply with all supervision conditions, including reporting, monitoring, and behavioral standards
• Failure to comply may result in revocation of release and re-detention
• Program modifications, escalations, or terminations may occur at any time based on compliance review or judicial instruction
• Continued participation is required for maintained release eligibility
Financial Responsibility & Payment Agreement
• All payments are applied toward pretrial supervision services, administrative processing, and program participation
• The guarantor or responsible party assumes full responsibility for accuracy, completion, and verification of all financial transactions
• Payments must clear prior to any release or service execution
• All financial transactions are logged, documented, and subject to audit and review
• Incorrect submissions may result in delays, reprocessing, or administrative hold
Once funds are processed and applied within authorized systems, they are subject to applicable administrative policies and are not guaranteed refundable except where required by governing regulations.
Payment Compliance & Verification Standards
• All payments must include accurate casefile identifiers and client credentials
• Identity verification is required for all payment methods
• Documentation of payment confirmation must be retained and submitted when requested
• Unauthorized payment activity, misrepresentation, or fraudulent submissions may result in immediate suspension of processing and referral to appropriate authorities
• Processing timelines vary based on verification procedures and system availability
Transport & Release Coordination Terms
• Transport services are coordinated following official release authorization only
• All transport actions must follow assigned routes, destinations, and supervision conditions
• Deviation or interference with assigned transport procedures may result in enforcement action
• Release processing timelines are subject to facility workload, verification clearance, and operational scheduling
• Completion of all required steps is mandatory prior to detainee release
Liability & Administrative Disclaimer
Oklahoma County Pretrial Services and associated departments operate under applicable state, county, and federal administrative frameworks. The department does not provide legal advice and assumes no liability for:

• User error during payment or submission processes
• Incorrect or incomplete case information provided by clients
• Delays caused by financial institutions or third-party processing systems
• Failure to follow instructions issued by authorized personnel
Acknowledgment & Consent
By engaging with pretrial services, submitting payments, or participating in any program or process, the individual acknowledges that they:

• Have read and understand all terms outlined on this page
• Are acting voluntarily and without coercion
• Accept responsibility for compliance with all procedures and requirements
• Understand that violations may result in administrative or legal consequences
